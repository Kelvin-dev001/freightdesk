How a job moves

One file follows the job from the BL to the director's desk.

  1. 1

    Create job file

    BL, vessel, ETA and container, entered once.

  2. 2

    Documents checklist

    Proforma to DO, ticked off as each one comes in.

  3. 3

    Tasks & port progress notes

    Who is on it, and where the file stands at the port.

  4. 4

    Expenses

    Port charges, interchange and more, each flagged billable or not.

  5. 5

    Invoice

    In KES or USD, with VAT at 16%.

  6. 6

    Payment reconciled

    M-Pesa, bank, cash or cheque, matched to the invoice.

  7. 7

    Transport & delivery

    Truck and driver allocated, trip expenses logged.

  8. 8

    Director dashboard

    Revenue, balances, profit and missing documents on one screen.

Questions before you book

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on your office PC, laptop or phone.

Can my staff have separate logins?

Yes, by role: operations, transport, finance and director. Each person sees what their role needs.

Can I use only finance or only transport?

Yes. Pick the modules you need, and pricing follows.

Is my data safe?

Hornbill is registered with the ODPC. Each person has their own login with role-based access, and the data sits on a managed cloud server, not on one office PC.

How long does setup take?

Most firms are up and running within a week. We load your first job files with you.

What happens to my existing Excel records?

We bring your active job files across with you during onboarding, and you can export to Excel any time.
